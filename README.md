# BUB1, BRCA1/2 and CDK Dysregulation in Oral Squamous Cell Carcinoma Progression
## Overview
This project investigates transcriptomic alterations during oral squamous cell carcinoma (OSCC) progression using RNA-seq differential expression analysis and biomarker validation workflows. The study compares normal gingival/buccal epithelial tissues, dysplastic lesions, and OSCC datasets to identify dysregulated mitotic and DNA repair biomarkers including BUB1, BRCA1/2, and CDK family genes.
## Background
Oral squamous cell carcinoma (OSCC) develops through progressive molecular alterations involving dysregulation of proliferation-associated and mitotic checkpoint pathways. This project investigates the progressive dysregulation of BUB1 and AURKA across normal oral mucosa, dysplasia, and OSCC.
## Objectives
- Identify differential expression of BUB1 and AURKA in OSCC
- Investigate biomarker progression from normal mucosa to dysplasia and carcinoma
- Evaluate pathways associated with proliferation and mitotic dysregulation
- Explore translational relevance of BUB1 and AURKA in OSCC
